Upstream port and voxel renderer

ZallaxDev and the Emerald 3DS contributors provided the original 3DS port,
voxel tools, and renderer. The recorded geometry sources are pinned to
b198c08defb94081ae995796cf75ab8cdb51f2d0.
https://github.com/ZallaxDev/emerald-3ds
Full scope and MIT text are in licenses/Emerald3DS-LICENSE-PORT.md.

The renderer incorporates logic from gradenGnostic/pokeemerald-multiplatform,
commit db1cab3d2dc9f0e0a9f4a3d67983e5acf30a9a46.
https://github.com/gradenGnostic/pokeemerald-multiplatform
The original MIT notice is in licenses/Voxel-renderer-MIT-NOTICE.md.

Linker source

sources/native.ld is the exact modified devkitARM 3dsx.ld source used to
link this build. Its modifications group game data and BSS before the
other native storage. This file is available under MPL 2.0, with the full
license in licenses/MPL-2.0.txt.
https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/

Game data and artwork

The small asset BPS files derive their sprite and palette banks from the
verified FireRed Evolved v0.2.31 output. Outdoor tile patterns use recorded
palette-index conversions from the same source game.
