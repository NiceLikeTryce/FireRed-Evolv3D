# FireRed Evolv3D

Major features

- All 251 Pokémon — Collect every Gen 1 and Gen 2 species on one save.
- Gen3 support — Trade, evolve, breed with incense and view National entries after Celio's Ruby/Sapphire quest.
- Save transfer — Import vanilla FireRed saves or move saves between both editions with an untouched backup.
- Bigger battle rewards — Earn bonuses and unlocks from Gyms and major battles.
- RNG Fix — Uses Ruby and Sapphire’s clock-based startup seeding.
- Voxel Kanto — Explore 3D scenery and battle scenes.
- Dual-screen controls — Use touch maps and menus on the bottom screen.
- Walking partners — Support all 386 species as walking partners.
- Battle Frontier — Challenge seven facilities and record battles.
- Secret Bases — Decorate, visit, and battle.
- Celadon Contests — Compete in five categories.
- Pokéblocks — Blend berries with NPCs or link partners.
- Berry gardening — Plant, water, and harvest berries.

Minor features

- Shared patcher hub — Select one ROM, choose GBA or 3DS, and read the controls guide and gameplay gallery.
- Browser patcher — Detect clean US/English FireRed v1.0 and v1.1 and assemble a verified 3DSX.
- A changing world — Real-time day and night and weather.
- Habitat Guide — Pokémon locations, odds, and unlock conditions.
- Training tools — IV and EV viewer, longer Repels, and faster egg hatching.
- Easier evolutions — Trade-backs, evolution stones, and missed-move learning.
- Tree Shaker — Find wild Pokémon in trees.
- Safari Zone — Catch all three Johto starters.
- Acro Bike — Hop ledges in either direction.
- Mirage Island — Take the ferry after the Champion.
- Camera controls — Choose Performance, Balanced or Quality; Performance supports Old 3DS.
- Optional challenges — Optional seeded randomizer and automatic Nuzlocke tracking; disabled by default.

Public platform source

- Native Nintendo 3DS platform port in progress.
- Input and video decoder bridge.
- Virtual GBA hardware memory for native game code.
- BIOS copies and immediate/deferred/repeating DMA.
- FireRed 640 and imported Emerald 512 bank selectors.
- Bounds-checked32-bit metatile attributes.
- Native FireRed engine-core provider interface.
- Synthetic host tests and diagnostic 3DSX build.

- Shared encounter rules and event scripts match FireRed Evolved.
- Listed rare species have 5%; nighttime grass and trees reserve 5% for Gastly and 1% for Haunter.

- Visible wild Pokemon — Optional encounters drawn from the normal area tables.
- Reward progress — Exact Mew/Celebi counts, missing species and habitat links.
- Save backups — Five verified snapshots per trainer with previews.
- Offline patching — Keep a verified package after initial setup.
- Modern menus — Current tabbed Options and reliable touch controls.
- Consistent presentation — Red Poke Ball startup, full intro and stable voxel recap/warp transitions.
